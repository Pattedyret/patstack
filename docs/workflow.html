Patstack, step by step

Patstack coordinates 19 callable skills under one lead. Pick a route and watch it move from intake to proof. Each step names what the agent does, the evidence it leaves behind and any gate a human keeps. This walkthrough illustrates the rules; it contains no measured results.

Route

    Full Send (default)

    The lead researches, makes routine choices and records them, then continues through PR, CI, the established test target and a live check.

    Full Stop

    For high-impact work the owner joins meaningful product and approach decisions. Work ends at an evidenced, ready, unmerged PR.

    R1 · R2 · R3

    Focused author check, one fresh opposite-model reviewer, or an independent Opus + Sol council. Chosen by behavior and blast radius.

    One repair flow

    PR and live failures return to the same task with a cumulative attempt count and clock. At most three normal cycles, then deep diagnosis.

    Keys: ← → step, space pauses. Under reduced-motion settings the walkthrough starts paused with no animation.
